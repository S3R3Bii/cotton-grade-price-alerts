# Earlier development check, before later-cohort scoring

Development uses 1,002 paired quote-days from Southeast and North Delta in the 2022–2023 and 2023–2024 source files. The fixed full grid contains 252,504 authored cases: two snapshot-expansion states, seven signed uniform changes, three identity states, two comparison rules and three alert thresholds. The independent checker verifies 3,584,316 exported conditions using separate exact formulas, without importing the analysis helpers.

Both rules and all thresholds remain retained. There are no learned coefficients, selected threshold, random search, tuned model or fitted forecast. Thresholds 1, 2 and 5 percent are authored sensitivity choices, not empirically optimized operating limits. Precision and recall are conditional on the equally enumerated case grid. Its positive-label shares are 6/7 at the 1 and 2 percent thresholds and 4/7 at 5 percent, not natural anomaly rates. Cases share source quotations and are not independent replications.

The matched rule's correct response when the unchanged full key remains available follows from the case construction and exact arithmetic; it does not establish superiority on live anomalies. Missing or changed identity requires abstention, which is distinct from a negative prediction. The row exports, market/signed-change breakdowns and calendar-month summaries preserve failures and denominators.

The next step is a committed dependency manifest before 2024–2025 all-market case summaries. Source/schema exposures are disclosed in SOURCE_BINDING.md; no analyst blindness is claimed. This is a controlled historical implementation study, not a naturally labeled prospective anomaly trial.
