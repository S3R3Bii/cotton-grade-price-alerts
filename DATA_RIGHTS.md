# Data scope and attribution

Source: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Marketing Service, Cotton and Tobacco Market News. The fixed requests use the official public historical upland price-file repository. USDA's digital-rights policy covers most government-produced information and preserves marked third-party restrictions: https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/policies-and-links .

Keep original factual spot quotations and source labels intact in the attributed projection. Derived summaries and every authored expansion, shock or identity-failure value are separately labeled as this study's calculations. Do not identify altered values as unchanged USDA data. Full workbooks, source pages/PDFs, logos, contacts, ICE futures values, private data and formal-publication drafts stay private. No USDA endorsement, commercial validation or unrestricted right to third-party source material is claimed.
