# Study 30: Keep cotton quality in a price alert

## Decision and acceptance

## Quality identity, source grain and eligibility

Target the two announced quote definitions: color 41 / leaf 4 / staple 34 and color 31 / leaf 3 / staple 36, across all seven source market areas, for each of the three named marketing-year files. Bind their exact labels/columns from the original headers and official meaning, preserving full raw source tokens and any other reported quality dimensions. Other quality dimensions must be equal or inherit an explicitly documented common quotation convention; do not infer physical equivalence of bales or erase a changing dimension to force a pair.

Require an absolute spot quotation in cents per pound, rather than an ICE settlement, basis-point quote or unconverted price differential. If a source uses differential plus base, permit reconstruction only when a source document explicitly states that arithmetic and both components are retained; otherwise leave units/meaning unknown and stop. Select no favorable date, premium or price range after values are seen. Retain all rows for the two planned definitions, every original copy and every rejection reason. Source weights and transaction counts do not become invented volume weights.

The unit is a market/date/complete-source-quality quotation, not a transaction, bale or independent farmer. Collapse only identical copies of the full key; conflicting copies make that key unavailable. Require finite positive baseline and finite nonnegative alternative quotations and unambiguous date/market/unit/quality identity. Missing grades are unknown, not zero. Holidays and nonpublication dates are not zero-price observations. Publish full available source date/market coverage and exact paired eligibility.

Before any benchmark score, require at least 100 paired quote-days in every source market for each marketing-year file. This minimum supports a bounded descriptive replay, not power or representativeness. If unmet or quality/unit identity remains unresolved, preserve the failure and stop this cohort rather than lower the gate after scores. Source identity and coverage are not labels of real market anomalies.

## Controlled cases and fixed comparators

For each eligible market/date pair, retain original base quotation B and added-quality quotation H. Each case starts with an old snapshot containing the base quote only. The new snapshot contains the same base and, in the expansion case, the additional quality. This is an authored snapshot transformation motivated by the documented format addition; it is not a capture of an actual before/after pipeline state.

Apply one prespecified uniform change s in {0,−0.10,−0.05,−0.02,+0.02,+0.05,+0.10} to the new snapshot's quotations, retaining original facts separately. Zero is a pure composition case. The same-quality truth is s by construction. Compare both no-expansion and added-quality snapshots, so true price changes and composition alone can be distinguished. Do not treat these authored multipliers as real price shocks, probability weights, customers' incidents or future behavior.

Two fixed identity rules: grade-blind averages all available quote rows in the new snapshot before comparing with old B; quality-matched compares the identical base quote definition and market. Use exact rational arithmetic on retained source decimal tokens. For each rule retain thresholds 1, 2 and 5 percent; an alert means absolute estimated change is at least the threshold, and the synthetic positive label is absolute s at least that same threshold. All six rule/threshold combinations remain visible; no threshold is learned or selected on development or held results. Precision/recall is conditional on this equally enumerated authored case grid and its exact labels, not estimated operational precision or natural anomaly prevalence. Undefined denominators remain unknown.

Separate missing-identity cases remove the base from the new snapshot or explicitly change its quality identity. The matched rule must abstain when identity is unavailable. Report abstention and coverage separately, retaining known authored truth for diagnosis; abstentions never count as correct negative predictions. Grade-blind may still emit a number, but that does not validate same-quality identification. Do not invent an undocumented grade crosswalk. If a deterministic key guard removes composition changes by construction, state that mathematical property; the data-specific observations are the source premium magnitudes and resulting false/missed alerts under these assumptions.
